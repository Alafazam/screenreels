Perform it once.

Every demo starts the same way: someone who knows the product uses it well, once. The recorder’s job is to be there when it happens — you click, type, and scroll; Studio writes the flow.

📖 Read the recording guide →

Try it: record against this page

Open Studio below, press Record, and use the form in the preview. When you stop, your session is a scene — every interaction an editable action.

What the recorder understands

A burst of keystrokes becomes one type action at your measured typing speed. A checkbox becomes toggle, a select becomes set, a slider becomes lever, a scroll burst becomes one relative scroll, your pauses become pacing — and a click that navigates becomes goto, with recording continuing on the new page. Password fields are never recorded.
