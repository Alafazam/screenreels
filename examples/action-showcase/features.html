Author

Record-by-doing

Open Studio, press Record, and use the preview like a normal page. Keystrokes coalesce into one type action at your typing speed, checkbox and select changes map to toggle/set, pauses become pacing, and a navigating click becomes goto.

Maintain

Flow doctor + CI

Demos drive the live app, so a renamed selector is the one way they rot. flow doctor --fix re-matches dead selectors by fingerprint, and CI annotates the PR that broke a demo. Real output:

$ screenreel flow doctor --flow demo.json --base-url http://localhost:4173 --fix ✖ tour-opening action 2 selector matches multiple elements repair .flow-row → [data-flow-step="1"] confidence 100 ▲ tour-controls action 5 has no matches (after a state-mutating action — dry-run warning, not repaired) 1 fix applied — re-run: ok: true
Produce

Voiceover

The talking points you already write become narration: screenreel assemble --voice speaks each scene over the captured video via macOS say, any TTS CLI, or your own engine. Preview the idea with your browser's voice:

The real feature renders AAC into the MP4 — this button is just your browser's speechSynthesis.

Personalize

Flow variables

Declare {{company}} once, personalize per prospect. One share link sets it for a whole run: ?demo=play&srv_company=…. Type a name and watch the flow use it:

Distribute

Share mode + analytics

Viewers get progress, play/pause, and exit — no presenter chrome. Playback emits a funnel (view_start → scene_enter → flow_complete / drop_off); watch it land in the panel above as demos play.

Engage

Viewer choice cards

A choice action shows up to four cards; clicking one jumps playback to that scene. Targets are graph-validated so a branch can never point at a missing or disabled scene.
