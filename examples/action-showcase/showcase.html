The journey of a demo.

Six chapters, six features — and the guided tour is the proof: press Play the journey and one flow will drive itself through every chapter page, doing each chapter’s thing as it passes through. Nothing on these pages is mocked.

How to read this story

Every chapter is a real, runnable demonstration. Follow them in order, or press play and let the tour take you — it types, highlights, branches, and hops pages on its own, emitting analytics the whole way.
