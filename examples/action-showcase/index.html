Why ScreenReel

A small contract,
not another platform.

01

Record by using your app

Studio watches trusted interactions and turns typing, selects, toggles, sliders, clicks, scrolling, and navigation into editable actions.

02

Commit a readable flow

The artifact is plain JSON in your repository: review it, diff it, version it, and keep every product surface under your control.

03

Keep it healthy

Validation catches broken targets. Fingerprints let flow doctor repair safe selector changes before a customer sees the break.

04

Bring your own stack

Apache 2.0, framework independent, no account, no required cloud service, and no data leaving the page unless you configure it.
